## 1. HTTP response — `status`

Stable · HTTP semantics and Google's redirect documentation · `technical-seo` · v2026.11

> Excellent = the final URL returns 200, no meta refresh, 10 hops or fewer (Googlebot follows up to 10;
> http-network-errors, 3xx section) · good = 200, but the final HTML has a meta refresh (Google treats it as a
> redirect) · none = the final URL returns 4xx (except 403), 5xx, or more than 10 hops · not rated = 403 or
> 429: may be a block on the inspector; if Googlebot gets the same, it is serious. The number of hops does not
> decide the level: it depends on how the URL is pasted. HTTPS is rated under "transport".

Caveat: "The time is one measurement from one place and is not rated: network timing varies with where it is
measured from."

Redirects no longer lower the label (v2026.10: "good = 200 after 1 redirect · low = 2 or more redirects").

```sh
curl -sIL 'https://zentimes.es/' | grep -iE '^(HTTP/|location:)'
```

Measured on the home page: **Excellent** · `200 · 25 ms` (EN run) / `200 · 9 ms` (ES run) · "Returns 200
directly."

## 3. robots.txt — search engines and assistants — `robots-busqueda`

Changing · the agent list and their roles change every few months · `technical-seo` · v2026.11

> On THIS page's PATH, for Googlebot, Bingbot, Applebot, OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User,
> PerplexityBot (Applebot without its own group inherits Googlebot's). Excellent = robots.txt returns 200,
> UTF-8, with those groups within the first 500 KiB, allows them this path and does not block the page's JS/CSS
> to Googlebot or Applebot · strong = allowed but only by omission (4xx except 429), or blocks the page's JS/CSS
> to Googlebot or Applebot, or not UTF-8, or some group sits past 500 KiB · low = Googlebot allowed, but one of
> the other 6 blocked · none = Googlebot blocked (not eligible for AI Overviews or AI Mode), all 7 blocked, or
> robots.txt returns 5xx or 429 · not rated = no response (may be an inspector failure). Sources: RFC 9309;
> Google's robots.txt spec (4xx except 429 = no file; content past 500 KiB is ignored; on 5xx it stops crawling
> for 12 h and uses the last good copy up to 30 days). Treating 429 as 5xx is an INFERENCE from two Google
> pages (robots.txt and http-network-errors), not from the RFC. Conventions: including Bingbot; "no robots.txt"
> = strong; Claude-User's weight. The rule says "allowed by robots.txt", not "can read".

Own grouping, seven agents, evaluated on the page's path (v2026.10: four agents on the root). ChatGPT-User and
Perplexity-User moved from this card to the "User:" line of card 4. Note that the printed command still tests
the root path `"/"` in `m(p)` while the rule says "THIS page's PATH"; for the home page the two coincide.


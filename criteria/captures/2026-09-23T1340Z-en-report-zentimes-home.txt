Transport security (HTTPS and HSTS)
Strong
max-age=31536000
HSTS max-age=31536000.
How to improve it
Add the header Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains. It forces the browser to always use HTTPS with your domain.
Stable
·
RFC 6797
Security, not legibility. Excellent = HTTPS with HSTS of at least one year and includeSubDomains · strong = HSTS of at least one year, no includeSubDomains · good = HSTS under one year · low = HTTPS without HSTS · none = no HTTPS. One year is the browsers' preload-list minimum.

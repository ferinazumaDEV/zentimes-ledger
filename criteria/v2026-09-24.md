## How a label is decided now

Three kinds of card decide a label, and a fourth prints a value with none. The kind is carried in the HTML as
`data-class` on `div.insp-item` and printed on the card next to the evidence class, e.g. `requisito ·
technical-seo · v2026-09-24`.

| `data-class` | cards | what it prints | `data-level` values it can carry |
|---|---|---|---|
| `requisito` | 12 | **Meets / Does not meet / Not applicable** | `cumple` · `no-cumple` · `no-aplica` (and `sin-evaluar` when it cannot be checked) |
| `regla` | 12 | a graded label, capped at good | `nula` · `baja` · `buena` · `sin-evaluar` (the rule text on every `regla` card names none, low, good and not rated only) |
| `percentil` | 2 | a graded label up to excellent | `nula` · `baja` · `buena` · `notable` · `excelente` · `sin-evaluar` |
| `informativa` | 10 | a value, no label, no `data-level` | — |

## Two copies of each card: the report is streamed

The report is no longer a single server-rendered document. The home-page cards arrive first, then the
site-wide part (B2, B3 and the requirements that depend on other pages) arrives while the page shows a
progress line ("4 requests to your server so far (cap 24) · 3 s of a maximum of 60 s. One request at a time,
at least 1 second apart, so your server is not overloaded."). In the served HTML this means that the cards
that can still change carry `data-provisional="true"` on their first copy and appear again, final, inside
hidden containers (`<div hidden id="S:…">`) that the page swaps in. In this capture 24 `div.insp-item` are
provisional and 36 are final; the final Summary and findings list are printed a second time after the footer.
Anyone reading the HTML with `grep` must skip the provisional copies: one of them (hreflang) printed
`Not rated` with "0 of 0 versions link back · 1 uncheckable" while its final copy prints `Meets` with "1 of 1
versions link back". The four cookbook-metric cards carry `data-metric`, `data-recipe` and `data-value` on both
copies with the same values.

Without JavaScript the page prints: "Without JavaScript this part does not update by itself. Reload the page
in a minute: the report is kept for 15 minutes and will come out complete." One inspection per origin every
10 minutes is the limit the operations side reports; a later request for the same origin returned the saved
report of the last run with a notice line, not a new inspection (observed on the ES page at 14:11Z, see the
end of this file).

### B4 · How it presents itself

**Page title** — `titulo` · `regla` · `technical-seo` · Stable ("Google's title-link documentation").
**Good** · `Zentimes — GEO: make your brand citable by AI` · ""Zentimes — GEO: make your brand citable by AI"

Brand"." Rule, verbatim:

> The title is split on space-surrounded separators (" | ", " – ", " — ", " - ", " · ", " : "), so "Coca-Cola"
> is not broken. Low = some segment is a vague label from the versioned list (Home, Inicio, Portada, Welcome…,
> Main page, Untitled, Index, WordPress's default tagline in English and Spanish), with a moderate finding
> (Google: "Avoid vague descriptors like "Home" for your home page"); or the title is just the site name
> (moderate, convention) · none = no title · good = otherwise. The site name comes from og:site_name, the
> WebSite name or, failing that, the domain. Limitation: it does not catch non-descriptive section titles.

## What can be said about the rules of this version

Derived from the history entries of v2026.09.1 and v2026.09.2 together, not from a capture of v2026.09.1
itself:

- The "excellent" definition of v2026.09.2 ("nothing verifiable is left to improve in that area") was introduced
  here. The eight additions named in the entry each map to a v2026.09.2 rule: HSTS (card 2), Open Graph
  (card 3), og:locale (card 4), hreflang return links (card 10), sitemap with this page and lastmod (card 16),
  linked JSON-LD graph (card 6), heading hierarchy without skips (card 11), unique ids (card 12).
- The image alt text check (card 13 in v2026.09.2) first appeared in this version.
- In this version HSTS was part of the **HTTP response** check, not a check of its own: the v2026.09.2 entry
  says HSTS "moves out of 'response' into its own check" and that, as a security practice, "it could not lower
  the HTTP response label". So under v2026.09.1 a page could be rated below excellent on HTTP response for
  lacking HSTS. Whether the transport check under v2026.09.2 has the same five steps HSTS had inside "response"
  under v2026.09.1: `needs-verification` (no capture).
- Words without JavaScript and the sameAs rule were left as in v2026.09, with the reason printed: "no data
  backs a higher bar".
